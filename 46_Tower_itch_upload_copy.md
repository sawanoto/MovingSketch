## Short description

Stack three-eyed Marpans, keep the tower balanced, and play a melody from bottom to top.

## Description (English)

Stack the colorful, three-eyed Marpans and build a tower of sound!

Each Marpan carries a musical note. Place them carefully, keep the tower from collapsing, and complete the melody. When the song is finished, your whole tower plays back from bottom to top.

Features:

- A playful mix of physics stacking and music
- Eight songs to unlock and complete
- Compose Mode unlocked after clearing every song
- Mouse, keyboard, and touch controls
- Japanese and English language support
- Runs directly in your browser

Controls:

- Mouse: Move to aim, click to drop
- Keyboard: Left / Right to move, Up to rotate, Space to drop
- Touch: Drag to aim, release to drop
- Round arrow button: Rotate
- Music-note button: Play the current tower

Can you keep the tower standing until the final note?
