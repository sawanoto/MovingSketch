## Title

Marpan Music Slot

## Short description

Stop Marpan's three spinning eyes, match the symbols, and turn every jackpot into a tiny music festival!

## Description (English)

Marpan's three eyes have become slot-machine reels!

Pull the lever, then tap each spinning eye to stop it. Match musical notes, stars, fruit, or the lucky 777 to earn notes and build your score. Hit the jackpot to enter FEVER mode, where rewards and scores are doubled for 10 spins.

You can also tap Marpan or play the little keyboard to collect notes, then spend them on new body colors.

Features:

- Three independently stoppable eye reels
- Timing bonuses for accurate stops
- 777 FEVER mode with doubled rewards
- A playable eight-key musical keyboard
- Unlockable Marpan colors
- English / Japanese language switch
- Mouse and touch controls
- No download required

How to play:

1. Pull the golden lever (costs 10 notes).
2. Tap the three eyes in any order to stop them.
3. Match symbols to win notes and score.
4. Tap Marpan or the keyboard whenever you need more notes.

Tip: Press D to toggle the hidden debug mode.
